📖 How to Play

Complete rules reference

🎯 Objective

🐺 Werewolves win by killing villagers until they equal or outnumber them. 👥 Village wins by eliminating all werewolves.

🎭 Roles

🐺 Werewolf tap to expand

Team: Wolf

Night: Kill 1 player (with fellow wolves)

Info: Knows all werewolves

Goal: Reach parity with village

🔮 Seer tap to expand

Team: Village

Night: Inspect 1 player → see exact role

Info: Most powerful village role

Goal: Guide village to correct eliminations

💊 Doctor tap to expand

Team: Village

Night: Protect 1 player → saves from wolf/sheriff kill

Note: Can self-save. Cannot save witch's poison.

🧙‍♀️ Witch tap to expand

Team: Village

Night: Sees wolf's target. Can heal wolf's target (1×) OR poison any player (1×)

Potions: 2 total — 1 heal, 1 poison. Each once per game.

Note: Can use both in one night. Poison cannot be saved.

⭐ Sheriff tap to expand

Team: Village

Night: Kill 1 player OR skip

Risk: Can accidentally kill a villager

Note: Can be saved by doctor, not by witch

👤 Villager tap to expand

Team: Village

Night: None

Day: Discuss + vote

Goal: Use deduction to find wolves

🌙 Night Phase (in order)

  1. 🐺 Werewolf — pick kill target
  2. ⭐ Sheriff — pick kill OR skip
  3. 🧙‍♀️ Witch — see wolf target, can heal/poison
  4. 💊 Doctor — pick protect target
  5. 🔮 Seer — inspect 1 player
  6. 🌅 Resolve — show deaths

☀️ Day Phase

  1. 📢 Show who died last night
  2. 💬 Discuss — share info, accuse, defend
  3. 🗳️ Vote — eliminate 1 player OR skip
  4. 🔁 Repeat night

🏆 Win Conditions

✅ Village wins: All werewolves eliminated

✅ Werewolves win: Alive wolves ≥ alive villagers

💡 Quick Tips

  • Never share your role unless it helps the village
  • Wolves lie — they can fake-claim any role
  • Watch who defends who — wolves protect each other
  • Dead players cannot vote or act

⚠️ Honor Rule

During night phase, keep your eyes closed unless your role is called. The app hides information on screen, but cannot stop you from peeking. Play fair — same as in-person Werewolf.
